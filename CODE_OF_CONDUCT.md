# Code of Conduct

Agent Jido Kata is a place for careful, useful engineering work. Participants
must make the project safe and practical for people with different experience,
backgrounds, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes.
- Protect private information and credentials.
- Respect source licenses, authorship, and project boundaries.
- Help maintainers keep discussions relevant and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publication of private information
are not acceptable. Repeated bad-faith arguments or attempts to bypass project
safety rules are also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `mike.hostetler@gmail.com`. The maintainers
can edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or limit
participation. They will consider context, impact, recurrence, and participant
safety. Do not retaliate against a person who reports a concern in good faith.
