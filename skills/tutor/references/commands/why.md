# Why

**Result:** A supported account of the purpose, rationale, evidence, and
tradeoffs that matter to the learner's use.

**Done:** The learner can state why the subject has this shape and which
material uncertainty remains.

Explain the problem that the design or action addresses. Distinguish documented
rationale, evidence-based inference, and unknown history. Keep confidence
language with the claim that it qualifies. Include a tradeoff only when it can
change the learner's decision or understanding.

Do not invent intent from current behavior. If rationale evidence is absent,
say what the design does and state that its original reason is unknown.
