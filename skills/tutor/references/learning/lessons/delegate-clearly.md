# Delegate clearly

**Practice:** Assign one output with context, authority, and return ownership.

Concepts: [authority](../concepts/authority.md) and
[ownership](../concepts/ownership.md).

1. Select one coherent result. Do not split work only to use more agents.
2. State the context, expected output, and completion evidence.
3. Separate allowed actions from decisions reserved for the human.
4. Name who performs, repairs, checks, and integrates the return.
5. Ask the learner to explain what the worker can decide without asking.

Worked example:

```text
Result: A table that compares three vendors on price, support hours, and
contract length, with one source for each cell.
Context: The team lead selects a vendor on Friday. The budget limit is in
the attached note.
Allowed: Read the vendor sites and the attached note.
Reserved: Do not contact a vendor. The team lead makes the selection.
Return to: Me. I check the sources and give the table to the team lead.
```

Common mistake:

```text
Look into these vendors and handle it.
```

"Handle it" does not say what to return or what the worker can decide. The
worker can contact a vendor or make the selection, and nobody is named to
check the return.

Complete when the learner can explain the assignment, its authority boundary,
and who receives the return. The lesson prepares a brief; it does not start a
worker.
