# Verify research or a recommendation

Use this method for research, analysis, recommendations, and decisions based on
sources.

1. State the decision or claim that the research must support.
2. Check that each material claim has a source and that the source directly
   supports it.
3. Prefer primary and current sources. Record dates for facts that can change.
4. Separate observed facts, calculations, source interpretations, and
   inferences.
5. Check important counterevidence and credible alternative explanations.
6. Recalculate important numbers from the supplied inputs.
7. Confirm that uncertainty and source limits are visible in the conclusion.

Return supported, unsupported, conflicting, and unknown claims. A reasonable
recommendation can still be unknown when the accountable value tradeoff is not
specified.
